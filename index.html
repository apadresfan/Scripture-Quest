How to Play

  • Voice Response: Tap the big gold microphone button and speak your answer clearly. Fuzzy match allows natural speech (e.g. saying "Jesus" or "option A").
  • Touch Response: Tap any of the option buttons directly.
  • 3 Strikes: You start with 3 lives. Each incorrect answer removes 1 life.
  • Progressive Difficulty: Get correct streaks to advance from Easy to Master!
  • Troubleshooting Mic/Speech: If speech stops working on Chrome or Mobile Safari, tap "🔊 Replay Voice" or toggle the Mic mode button.
